Our Products

Ten key raw materials for paint and coatings manufacturers. Tap Enquire on any product to request a quote.

Caustic Soda

Sodium Hydroxide (NaOH)

Adjusts pH and neutralises acidity in water-based paints, putties and coatings.

Typical users: Paint, putty, distemper and detergent manufacturers.

Starch

Modified / Native

Low-cost thickener and binder that adds body to distempers, putties and adhesives.

Typical users: Budget paint, wall putty and adhesive makers.

Dry Film

Film Former Additive

Helps paint form a tougher dry film, supporting gloss, durability and weather resistance.

Typical users: Exterior, industrial and protective coating producers.

Dispersing Agent

Pigment Dispersant

Keeps pigments and fillers evenly spread, giving stronger colour and better stability.

Typical users: Emulsion, enamel and industrial paint manufacturers.

Ammonia

pH Adjuster

Raises and stabilises pH in emulsion paints and latex systems during production.

Typical users: Water-based paint and coatings plants.

IGSURF

Surfactant

Wetting and emulsifying agent that improves pigment wetting and formulation stability.

Typical users: Emulsion paint and specialty coating formulators.

Defoamer

Foam Control

Breaks and prevents foam during mixing, filling and application for a smooth finish.

Typical users: Virtually every water-based paint manufacturer.

HEC

Hydroxyethyl Cellulose

Standard thickener for emulsion paints, controlling viscosity, flow and water retention.

Typical users: Emulsion paint, putty and texture coating makers.

PU Thickener

Associative Rheology Modifier

Gives premium paints smooth levelling, good roller flow and low spatter.

Typical users: Premium decorative and wood coating producers.

Texanol

Coalescent

Helps latex particles fuse into a continuous film, including at lower temperatures.

Typical users: Premium emulsion and exterior paint manufacturers.

Grades, pack sizes and TDS/COA available on request.
